SuperMod layers match SuperMod's current release. The Belaya, Chornivsk, Hrodna Border and Rassvet maps are added, and
the Johat, Khokhan, Mogadishu and OP First Light layers that SuperMod no longer ships are removed.

Each SuperMod layer takes its map and gamemode from SuperMod's own data, which is what the game loads. A GoingDark or
Large variant shares its map with the layer it is built on: _SU_GoingDark_Tallil_RAAS_v1_ is on Tallil, and
_SU_Chora_LargeInvasion_v1_ on Chora. _SU_Tallil_SeedVehicle_v1_ is a Seed layer, _SU_GoingDark_Narva_RAAS_v1_ a RAAS
PreCap layer, and _SU_GoingDark_Narva_RVAAS_v1_ a RAAS layer.

GoingDark layers, SuperMod's night layers, carry a GD version, such as V1GD for _SU_GoingDark_Narva_RAAS_v1_, the way a
world-partitioned layer carries WP. A filter on the version can include or exclude them.

Some vehicles that only SuperMod ships are renamed to the names SuperMod now gives them, such as _Mi8 CAS_ for _Mi-8
CAS_. A filter on a vehicle's old name no longer matches it, so check vehicle filters for SuperMod vehicles.

Vehicles that vanilla Squad also ships take their class from vanilla Squad. The BRDM-2 is a light vehicle and the
Technical BMP-1 a tank destroyer again, and the CH-146 CAS is an attack helicopter.
