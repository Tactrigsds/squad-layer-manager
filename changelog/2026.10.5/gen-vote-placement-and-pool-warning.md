A vote can be saved without a false _Filter Warnings_ alert. The alert called the vote's default choice "not in the pool" even when the choice was in it, so the vote could only be saved with _Save Anyway_. When a vote is the next layer, the in-game next layer message to admins now lists the repeat rules and pool filters that the vote's current choice breaks.

In _Gen Vote_, clicking the placement option that is already selected keeps it selected. Clicking it used to select the other option.
