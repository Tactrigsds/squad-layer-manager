SLM no longer ships the Balance Triggers plugin. Balance trigger alerts stop appearing on the dashboard, in match
history and in post-roll warns.

An upgrade keeps the plugin's settings and recorded trigger events where any were recorded. The Plugins section of the
settings page lists them as leftover data, and an admin can delete them there. Where no event was ever recorded, the
upgrade deletes the plugin's settings.
