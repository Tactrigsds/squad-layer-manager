Squads show their kills, wounds, deaths and K/D in the teams panel and in a past match's teams view.

The _K/W/D_ sort in the teams panel sorts players, or squads by their players' combined score, by kills, wounds,
deaths, K/D or W/D. A squad's score shows on its header row while spoilers are shown. A past match's teams view has the
same sort options, sorts by name or time on team as well, and has a search box that filters its players. Its
_Counted only_ checkbox hides the players the team breakdown does not count.
