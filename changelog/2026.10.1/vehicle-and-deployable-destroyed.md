The activity feed shows vehicles and deployables being destroyed, with who destroyed them and who was aboard, and FOB radios coming under attack.

Destroyed vehicles and deployables appear in the Killfeed filter. Attacks on FOB radios appear in the Default filter: once when an attack starts, and once when the radio is taken down to its minimum health.

History can search them by the vehicle, the vehicle or deployable type, or the exact blueprint.

None of these are recorded on training layers such as Jensen's Range. The server setting "Skip Destroyed Vehicles on Training
Layers" turns that off.
