The server dashboard on a phone now fits the screen, and a long press opens the menu of a match or a layer.

- The Matches and Teams screens no longer scroll sideways or cut off on the right. Long squad names are shortened instead.
- On the Activity screen, the event filter is placed beside the chat channel picker, so the header takes one line.
- While a text field is focused, the top bar and the tab bar are hidden, which leaves more room above the on-screen keyboard.
- A long press on a match in match history opens its menu. Matches can no longer be dragged on a phone, since there is nowhere to drop them.
- A long press on a layer in the layer picker's results opens its menu.
