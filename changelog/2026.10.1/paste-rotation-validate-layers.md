SLM now checks every line pasted into _Paste Rotation_ before adding any layer.

Each line that fails the check is listed with the layer, faction or unit SLM does not recognise, and the closest name
it does recognise. A faction or unit its layer cannot be played with is named too. Nothing is added until every listed
line is fixed or removed. Turn off _Validate layers_ to add such lines anyway, as raw layers.
