The activity feed folds repeated combat events from one player into one expandable row. A gunner's run of hits on one vehicle, a multi-kill with one weapon, and several deployables destroyed with one weapon each show as one row.

The row sums up the run, such as "hit BTR-80 12 times for 340". Expand it to see each event. Events join a row while they keep arriving less than 5 seconds apart. Warns that share their text and sender now fold the same way, and a folded warn row now updates its count as more warns arrive.
