The _Scoreline_ chart is now called _Scores_ and plots tickets by default. The _Population_ chart's _Teams_ view plots how many more players one team had than the other.

The _Population_ chart's _Stats_ for a single match cover the round. They show the player count at the round's start
and end, and the joins and leaves during the round.

The scoreboard plugin's Discord post includes a kill lead chart, between the kills and tickets charts.
