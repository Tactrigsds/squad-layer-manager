Conditions in the filter editor are added and changed from one searchable menu. The menu sorts conditions into _Block_, _Layer_, _Faction_, _Vehicle_ and _Scores_ tabs, and opens on the tab of the condition being changed.

Any condition can be changed into any other kind. A condition keeps what still applies after the change: a block keeps its conditions, a matchup keeps its teams, and a faction or unit condition passes its values to a matchup and back. A condition that becomes a block is kept inside the new block.

_Included in filter_, _excluded from filter_, _allow matchups_ and _disallow matchups_ are separate entries in the menu. A matchup starts with only its faction and unit fields. Use _+ dimension_ to add alliance or vehicle fields.

A condition's collapsed row lists every value it matches, and wraps onto more lines when the values do not fit. _Select layers_ conditions list their layers instead of a count. The save, clone and delete buttons sit beside the filter's description, so the conditions use the full width of the page.
