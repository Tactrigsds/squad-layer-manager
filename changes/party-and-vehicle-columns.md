The teams panel shows the in-game party each player queued with, and the vehicle and seat they are in.

The Party column can be filtered and sorted. Shift+click a party cell to select that party's members on the same team, or Shift+Ctrl+click to select them on both teams. The player context menu offers the same selection.

Party is also a grouping mode. Pick Party under Grouping mode to color players by party and split the team breakdown chart by party. Players outside a party fall under No party.

The Vehicle column is a spoiler, shown with Show Spoilers. The player details window shows both the party and the vehicle.
