The scoreboard's _Squad Kills_ leaderboard lists each squad by its number, the player who created it and its faction, such as "Squad 3 (grey, USMC)", in place of the squad's name.

The _FOB/HAB Destroyer_ leaderboard is renamed _FOB Destroyer_. It ranks players by the damage they dealt to enemy FOB radios, and no longer counts HABs.
