A new AFK Kicker plugin kicks AFK players when people are waiting in the queue.

A player is AFK after five minutes out of a squad. On seeding and training layers, it is fifteen minutes without doing
anything instead. It warns AFK players while the server is full, and gives each one a final warning five seconds before the
kick. It is off until you pick the servers it runs on.
