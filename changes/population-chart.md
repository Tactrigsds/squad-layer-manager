A new _Population_ chart in the _Charts_ panel plots how many players were on the server, split into active and idle
players or by team.

The chart covers the match the panel displays, or every match in the last 6 hours, day or week. A player in a squad or
in a vehicle is never idle. Anyone else is idle after a set time without doing anything, 10 minutes by default. Click a
match on the chart to open it. The chart scales to the server's player cap, or to the players drawn. Pick _Stats_ for
the peak, lowest, average and median player count, the time spent full, the share of idle players, the gap between
the teams, joins and leaves per hour, and the churn rate over the same time. A chart's own window now opens at the size the chart has in the panel, and the panel shows a
note with a _Return to panel_ button while the chart is out.
