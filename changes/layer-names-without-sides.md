A layer that is not tied to a match shows its teams in the layer's own order, without (1)/(2) or (A)/(B) markers. That covers layers picked into a filter, the layer history window, and activity feed lines about queue edits, votes and fulfilled requests. Feed lines about a next layer set on the server mark its teams with the sides of the match it is set for, instead of sides that were sometimes wrong.
